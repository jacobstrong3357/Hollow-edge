BEFORE YOU BEGIN

How to Play

I
THE GOAL

One of your eight neighbours is not what they seem. Learn who it is, and what horror wears their face, then accuse them so the rite can end it.

II
THE RHYTHM

Two actions by day: question a villager, search a place, examine the dead. By night, bar your door, watch someone's, or walk the dark yourself. Hunt it too openly and it hunts you back.

III
WINNING AND LOSING

Name the right person AND the right creature together and it ends. Get either wrong and an innocent may hang, or you might. Let too many nights pass and the village runs out of people.

IV
IF YOU GET STUCK

The Journal, at the bottom of the screen, keeps your signs, your struck-off suspects, and a full Help tab with the fine print.
